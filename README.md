# [TrafficSignal]: AI for faster road issue reporting

Client: City of San José, Community Safety
Team: Ahmed Al-Tameemi, ArvinJohn Vinluan, Nancy Kherella, Russell Jonathan Estrada, Sofia Amir, Aug–Oct 2026
My part:  I developed the feasibility and data analysis, evaluated AI outputs, and helped assess AI governance and risk.

## The problem
San José residents encounter road issues such as potholes, damaged infrastructure, unsafe road conditions, and speeding, but reporting them can be confusing and time-consuming. Existing reporting processes can make it difficult for residents to know where and how to report an issue while giving city staff inconsistent information to review.

## What we built
- An AI-assisted reporting prototype that makes it easier for residents to report road hazards, speeding concerns, and other street-related issues.

- A simple interface that helps users describe and categorize an issue and provide the information the city needs to respond.

- AI assists with organizing and routing reports, while city staff remain responsible for reviewing reports and deciding what action should be taken.

## What I would do next
I would improve the prototype by connecting it with real City of San José reporting workflows and data, then measure whether it reduces reporting time, improves report quality, and helps city staff respond more efficiently.
